title: "What I throw away before the model ever sees it"
stream: notes
date: 2026-01-22
tags: [signal-processing, eeg]
excerpt: "Most of the effort in a signal pipeline isn't the classifier. It's deciding what not to classify."
---

Working with EEG data teaches you fast that the interesting part isn't the model — it's everything upstream of it. Raw signal is mostly noise wearing a costume: muscle artifacts, electrode drift, 60Hz hum, blink spikes that look for a moment like intent.

Every hour spent tuning a classifier is worth less than an hour spent deciding what never reaches it.
